# TheMossHead-s-Academics
TheMossHead’s Academics is a free study app for Year 10 exam prep. It covers Maths, Science, English, French and Legal Studies with over 11,000 flashcards, topic sheets, past exam papers, a 217-question topic test, a triage tool that ranks what to study first, exam dates and a full-screen flashcard focus mode. Works on phone and laptop.
